My Apple Pie Reviews

Here are all the apple pies I have tried. Each review includes photos, my detailed thoughts, and my rating using the 5-point system. I will keep adding new reviews as I discover more pies.

RATING

Citi Market

This apple pie is amazing. The crust is perfectly flaky and golden. When you bite into it, it cracks just right. The apples are fresh and you can taste each slice. The sweetness is perfect, not too much sugar, just enough to bring out the apple flavor.

Would I buy it again?
Yes, absolutely!

Total Score: 5 / 5

Costco

This apple pie is good but not great. The crust visually looks nice. However, the top and bottom crust is a little too thick. The apples taste okay, but they are very soft, almost like applesauce. The pie is very sweet and requires ice cream to balance out the sweetness.

Would I buy it again?
Maybe, if nothing else is available.

Total Score: 2 / 5
